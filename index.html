Privacy Policy

Quran E Karim · Last updated 12 November 2026

Quran E Karim has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We, the app's developer, do not receive or store any of your personal information. Everything you save in the app stays on your device.

1. Information kept on your device

The app saves the following on your phone only, so it is there the next time you open the app:

  • the name you enter on the Profile screen (optional)
  • bookmarks, notes and memorization progress
  • reading history, reading progress and your last-read position
  • your settings, and the place you chose for prayer times

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can also edit or delete bookmarks and notes in the app at any time.

2. Location

The app asks for your location only if you tap Use my location on the Prayer screen. Your location is then:

  • rounded to about 1 km,
  • sent to AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com) to calculate that day's prayer times, and
  • saved on your device so you don't have to share it again.

Location is not used for anything else and is never collected in the background. If you prefer, you can type a city instead; the city and country are then sent to AlAdhan in the same way. You can turn off the app's location permission in your device settings at any time.

3. Services the app connects to

The Quran text, translations, tafsir, recitations and prayer times are downloaded from these services when you use the app:

  • AlQuran.cloud (api.alquran.cloud) and the Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network): Quran text, most translations, search and recitation audio. When you search, your search words are sent to AlQuran.cloud.
  • Quran.com (api.quran.com): some translations, word-by-word meanings and tafsir.
  • QuranEnc.com (quranenc.com): tafsir in Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Bengali and Telugu, and translation notes in Kannada and Marathi.
  • AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com): prayer times, as described above.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): the Arabic and Urdu fonts.

As with any internet request, these services receive your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as the type of device) needed to send the content. Their own privacy policies apply to that information. The app does not send them your name, notes, bookmarks or any identifier.

4. Sharing and feedback

When you use Share, the app opens your device's share menu; what you share, and with whom, is your choice. Feedback opens your email app, and we only receive what you choose to send.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or analytics tools.
  • We don't create accounts or profiles of users.

6. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Security

All of the app's connections use encrypted HTTPS. Information saved on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact email shown on Quran E Karim's Google Play page.
